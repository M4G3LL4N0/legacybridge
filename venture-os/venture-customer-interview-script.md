# Venture Customer Interview Script

## Interview Goal
Validate pain, workaround, and willingness to take next step

## Who To Interview
Operations leaders and technical founders

## Opening
"I'm exploring Teams cannot verify identity, claims, or risk fast — not selling yet. Can I ask how you handle this today?"

## Problem Questions
- When did this last happen?
- What did it cost you?
- What did you try?

## Current Workflow Questions
- Walk me through your current process step by step
- What tools do you use?

## Pain Questions
- What's most frustrating?
- What happens if you do nothing?

## Budget / Value Questions
- What would fixing this be worth per month?
- Have you paid for tools in this category before?

## Existing Alternatives Questions
- What else did you consider?
- Why didn't that work?

## Demo Reaction Questions
(if demo shown)
- What would you use this for first?
- What's missing?

## Closing Ask
"Can I follow up with a demo link / early access when ready?"

## Strong Signal Answers
Specific recent example, quantified pain, active search for solution

## Weak Signal Answers
"Vague sometimes" — no recent example

## Red Flags
No pain, no budget authority, wants free forever, unrelated use case
