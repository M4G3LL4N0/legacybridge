# Venture Channel Map

## Best First Channel
Manual outreach

## Why This Channel
Operations leaders and technical founders likely discover via manual outreach given product type and stage

## Channel Options
SEO: prep first
Content: prep first
Launch: prep first
Manual outreach: not yet
Community: test after content
Social: LinkedIn
Referral: after core loop
Lead magnet: optional
Partnership: explore after signal
Marketplace: later
Paid channel: no — organic first

## Channels To Avoid For Now
Paid ads, mass email, automated DMs, spammy communities, fake urgency launches

## Channel Risk
Wrong audience, weak landing page, promoting before demo works, vanity metrics

## Channel Success Signal
Qualified waitlist, reply with specific pain, demo booked, quote with budget

## Channel Stop Rule
Stop channel after 30 days or 100 impressions with zero qualified signups/replies
