# Venture Revenue Signal Capture

## Revenue Signal Goal
Prove buyer intent for Demo: System intelligence for legacy software

## Strong Revenue Signals
Quote request with budget · Paid pilot interest · Deposit interest · Payment intent · Actual payment

## Weak Revenue Signals
Vague interest · Likes · Pricing curiosity without use case

## Revenue Signal Sources
Quote form · pricing click · paid pilot request · manual conversation · invoice request

## Fields To Capture
Offer · Price · Budget · Timeline · Buyer · Use case · Urgency · Payment intent · Objection · Next step

## Signal Score Rule
Strong = budget + timeline + buyer identified (75+) · Medium = interest without budget (40-74) · Weak = curiosity only

## Best Revenue Signal Action
Add quote request CTA fields to form map — log manually until Stripe approved
