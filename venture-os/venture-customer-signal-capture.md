# Venture Customer Signal Capture

## Customer Signal Goal
Prove Operations leaders and technical founders will engage with Demo: System intelligence for legacy software

## Strong Customer Signals
Books demo, asks detailed workflow questions, requests follow-up or trial access

## Weak Customer Signals
Generic signup, vague compliment, no use case, no reply to follow-up

## Customer Signal Sources
Waitlist · feedback form · interview · demo request · quote request · email reply · manual note

## Fields To Capture
Customer segment · Pain · Use case · Urgency · Current workaround · Desired outcome · Budget · Objection · Next step

## Signal Score Rule
Strong = use case + budget/timeline + reply (80+) · Medium = use case + reply (50-79) · Weak = generic interest (<50)
