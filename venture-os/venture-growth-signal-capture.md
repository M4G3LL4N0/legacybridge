# Venture Growth Signal Capture

## Growth Signal Goal
Prove repeatable channel brings qualified leads

## Strong Growth Signals
Qualified lead · Demo request · Quote request · Return visit · Referral · Reply with pain · Targeted waitlist

## Weak Growth Signals
Generic views · Impressions · Likes · Unrelated traffic · Bot traffic

## Growth Signal Sources
SEO · landing page · content · launch · manual outreach · community · referral · portfolio cross-link

## Fields To Capture
Channel · Source · Audience · CTA · Action · Intent · Quality · Next step

## Signal Score Rule
Strong = qualified action from target segment (70+) · Medium = click without form (30-69) · Weak = vanity (<30)

## Best Growth Signal Action
Track channel + outcome for next 10 visits — log in markdown
