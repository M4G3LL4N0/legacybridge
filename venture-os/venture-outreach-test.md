# Venture Outreach Test

## Outreach Audience
Operations leaders and technical founders

## Outreach Channel
Email, LinkedIn, warm intro — founder manual only

## Outreach Angle
Teams cannot verify identity, claims, or risk fast enough for high-stakes decisions.

## Message Version 1
Hi — I'm testing System intelligence for legacy software for Operations leaders and technical founders. If Teams cannot verify identity, claims, or risk fast resonates, open to a 15-min problem chat?

## Message Version 2
Softer: "Researching how teams handle Teams cannot verify identity, claims, or — would value your perspective."

## Message Version 3
Direct: "We built a demo for System intelligence for legacy software — want a 10-min walkthrough?"

## What To Ask For
Reply, problem interview, or demo — not payment yet unless paid signal mode

## Strong Signal
Books demo, asks detailed workflow questions, requests follow-up or trial access

## Weak Signal
Generic signup, vague compliment, no use case, no reply to follow-up

## Do Not Do
Do not spam. Do not misrepresent status. Do not claim traction. Do not overpromise. No automatic sends.
