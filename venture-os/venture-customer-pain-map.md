# Venture Customer Pain Map

## Main Pain
Teams cannot verify identity, claims, or risk fast enough for high-stakes decisions.

## Trigger Moment
When Teams cannot verify identity, claims, or risk fast blocks progress or creates visible cost

## Current Workaround
Manual tools, spreadsheets, generic SaaS, or doing nothing

## Why Current Options Are Bad
Too generic, too slow, or not built for AI agents

## Desired Outcome
System intelligence for legacy software

## Emotional Driver
Frustration with wasted time and uncertainty

## Economic Driver
Save time, reduce risk, or capture revenue from AI agents

## Proof Needed
User completes demo path and submits request or saves an artifact

## Validation Question
"How much did this cost you last month in time or money, and what did you try instead?"
