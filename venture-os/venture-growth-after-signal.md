# Venture Growth After Signal

## If Strong Signal
Improve landing funnel conversion

## If Medium Signal
Improve landing page + message; retest same channel

## If Weak Signal
Revise angle or audience; do not scale spend

## If No Signal
Stop channel after 30 days or 100 impressions with zero qualified signups/replies

## Build Only After Growth Signal
Dedicated landing variants, email nurture, second channel, paid test (approval only)

## Do Not Build Yet
Paid ads, automation sequences, affiliate program, full SEO farm
