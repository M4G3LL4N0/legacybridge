# Venture Investor Claim Safety

## Safe Claims
- Built prototype / live MVP at https://legacybridge.noaerth.com
- Build status: in recovery
- Stage: STAGE_3_BUILDABLE
- Target customer: Operations leaders and technical founders
- Venture-os documented execution

## Risky Claims
- "Demo-ready" without live walkthrough
- "Validation-ready" without logged test
- Any specific market size without source

## Unsupported Claims
- Revenue-generating
- Profitable
- Paying customers
- Active users
- Trusted by
- Enterprise-ready
- SOC 2 compliant
- Funded
- Partnered with
- Market leader
- Proven traction

## Required Evidence Before Claiming
Customer interview log; quote request; payment; third-party audit

## Safer Alternatives
- Instead of "customers" → "design partner conversations in progress"
- Instead of "revenue" → "first-dollar path identified"
- Instead of "enterprise-ready" → "early B2B prototype"
