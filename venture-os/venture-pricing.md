# Venture Pricing

**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-22

## Pricing Hypothesis
Design partners pay for time-to-outcome in ai agents — not feature count.

## Suggested Pricing Tiers
- **Free:** Public site + limited demo / waitlist  
- **Starter:** $29–$49/mo or pilot flat fee (1 seat)  
- **Pro:** $99–$199/mo (small teams)  
- **Business:** $499+/mo (workflows + support)  
- **Enterprise:** Custom (after proof)

## Value Metric
Seats or active projects

## First Paid Offer
Fix product surface, then sell a 2-week paid pilot to 1 design partner

## Pricing Risks
Charging before proof event exists; pricing above incumbent without 10× wedge clarity.

## Pricing Experiments
Founding plan, annual prepay discount, paid pilot → convert to subscription.
